Real Estate Boom: Metro Cities Witness Rapid Growth

Anil Kapoor

November 21, 2024

Urban centers attract record investment in residential projects

India's real estate sector is experiencing unprecedented growth, with metro cities witnessing massive investment influx. Commercial and residential projects worth ₹2.5 lakh crores are under development across major cities.

Market Overview: • Delhi NCR: ₹75,000 crore projects in pipeline • Mumbai: ₹60,000 crore new residential projects • Bangalore: ₹50,000 crore IT and commercial zones • Hyderabad: ₹45,000 crore infrastructure development • Average property price appreciation: 12% annually

Experts attribute this boom to improved infrastructure, remote work flexibility, and increased foreign investment. NRI investors are particularly interested in premium residential segments.

Government incentives including GST reduction and faster approvals have accelerated project launches. Real estate consultants predict this trend will continue, with property values expected to appreciate 15% annually over the next three years. However, affordable housing remains a challenge, with government emphasizing the need for more budget-friendly projects.
